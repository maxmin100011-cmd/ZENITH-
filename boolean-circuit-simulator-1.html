How this works

  • Drag a gate, switch, or output from the left onto the canvas.
  • To wire two things together, either: drag from one dot to another, or tap the output dot (it starts glowing) then tap the input dot you want it to reach — no dragging needed.
  • Prefer typing? Use the "From / To" dropdowns above the canvas and press Connect. The list below it shows every wire in plain words, with a ✕ to remove one.
  • Tap an input switch (A, B, C, D) to flip it between 0 and 1 — connected wires light up instantly.
  • Tap a gate or wire to select it, then use Delete in the toolbar to remove it.
  • Double-tap a gate to rename its label.
  • Open Truth table to see every input combination, or try Challenge mode to test yourself.
